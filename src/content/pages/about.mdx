## Our Mission

In an age of hot takes and engagement bait, we believe there's still a place for thoughtful, long-form writing. Our essays aim to go beyond the surface—to examine not just what's happening in technology, but why it matters for how we live.

We're not interested in reviewing the latest gadgets or breathlessly covering funding rounds. Instead, we explore the deeper currents: the attention economy, digital citizenship, the aesthetics of the internet, and the human cost of automation.

## What We Cover

* **Technology & Society** — How digital tools reshape communities, institutions, and democracy
* **Attention & Time** — The economics of our most precious cognitive resource
* **Work & Identity** — What automation means for how we find meaning and purpose
* **Digital Culture** — The aesthetics, memes, and movements that define life online

## Who We Are

Digital Divide is written and edited by a small team of writers, researchers, and technologists who believe that nuance is not a weakness but a strength.

We're independent and reader-supported. We don't run ads or sell your data. If you find value in our work, consider [subscribing to our newsletter](/subscribe).
