Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

## Information We Collect

### Newsletter Subscription

When you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect your email address. This information is stored with our email service provider, MailerLite, and is used solely to send you our newsletter.

### Analytics

We use privacy-focused analytics to understand how visitors use our site. This may include information like pages visited, time on site, and general geographic location (country level). We do not track individual users or use cookies for analytics purposes.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

* Send you our newsletter (if you've subscribed)
* Understand how our site is used so we can improve it
* Respond to your inquiries if you contact us

## What We Don't Do

We do not:

* Sell your personal information
* Share your email with third parties (except our email provider)
* Use tracking cookies or fingerprinting
* Display personalized advertising

## Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may collect data:

* **MailerLite** — For newsletter management. [View their privacy policy](https://www.mailerlite.com/legal/privacy-policy)

## Your Rights

You have the right to:

* **Unsubscribe** from our newsletter at any time using the link in any email
* **Request deletion** of your personal data by contacting us
* **Request access** to the personal data we hold about you

## Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or your personal data, please contact us at [privacy@digitaldivide.com](mailto:privacy@digitaldivide.com).

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Significant changes will be announced via our newsletter or on this page.
