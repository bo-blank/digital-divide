Noticed something this morning: I reached for my phone before I even opened my eyes. The first thing my brain wanted wasn't coffee, or the sunlight through the window, or even a coherent thought—it wanted the dopamine hit of notifications.

We've trained ourselves to be interrupted. The question is whether we can unlearn it.
