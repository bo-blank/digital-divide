Started keeping a mental list of phrases that make me suspect I'm reading AI-generated text:

- "It's important to note that..."
- "Let's dive in!"
- "In today's fast-paced world..."
- "Whether you're a beginner or an expert..."
- "At the end of the day..."
- Lists of exactly five items, all starting with the same part of speech

The thing is, humans use these phrases too. We always have. But AI uses them with a particular density and randomness—sprinkled throughout like someone followed a recipe without understanding why each ingredient was there.

There's also the voice problem. AI prose is fluent but somehow voiceless. It reads like text that was *produced* rather than *written*. The difference is hard to articulate but easy to feel. Writing has fingerprints. AI text has been wiped clean.

I don't think detection will stay easy. The models are getting better fast. But right now, in this particular moment, there's still something uncanny about machine prose—a smoothness that registers as wrongness, a competence that reads as hollow.

We're developing a new kind of literacy, whether we realize it or not.
